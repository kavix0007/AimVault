What Is TenZ's Valorant Crosshair?

TenZ is the online name of Tyson Ngo, a Canadian content creator and former professional VALORANT player. His small cyan crosshair is widely recognized and is often used as a starting point by players who prefer a compact, clean sight picture.

AimVault lists the current snapshot above and gives the exact share code alongside the settings that make up the profile. Because professional players can change settings, this page includes a verification date instead of presenting the configuration as permanent.

Why Do Players Use the TenZ Crosshair?

The profile is compact: short inner lines, no visible center dot, no outer lines and no movement or firing error indicators. That leaves the center of the screen relatively clear while keeping a small aiming reference visible around the target.

A pro crosshair will not automatically improve your aim. Use the TenZ profile as a baseline, then adjust the length, thickness, offset or color if the crosshair does not feel comfortable for your eyes and playstyle.

TenZ Crosshair Settings Explained

  • Cyan: a bright color intended to remain easy to see against many backgrounds.
  • Inner lines: short 2-length, 2-thickness lines with a 1 offset.
  • Center dot: disabled, keeping the middle of the crosshair open.
  • Outer lines: disabled for a smaller visual footprint.
  • Movement and firing error: disabled for a static crosshair.

Is TenZ's Crosshair Good for Beginners?

It can be a good starting point if you like small static crosshairs. Beginners should focus more on consistent crosshair placement and practice than on copying a professional player's exact setup.
