Best Dot Crosshair Settings for Valorant

Dot crosshairs strip everything down to a single point. Here's when that works well, and a few AimVault codes to try.

Why players use a dot

A dot crosshair removes the outer lines entirely, leaving one small marker at the center of the screen. That means nothing extra covers your target, which is useful for headshot-focused playstyles where precision matters more than a large visual anchor.

What to watch out for

Because a dot is small, it can be harder to track during fast movement or in low-contrast areas of a map. If you find yourself losing the dot on certain maps, try a slightly larger dot size or a brighter color rather than switching back to a full cross.
